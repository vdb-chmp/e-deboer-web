ABOUT

A hands-on practice built around atmosphere, material and the physical presence of objects in space.

E. DE BOER is a Spain-based visual stage artist and prop & scenic fabricator, developing handcrafted objects and environments for visual spaces.

The practice is rooted in making: working with scale, natural forms, aged surfaces, textiles, wood, metal and botanical material to create pieces that feel tactile, lived-in and specific to their setting.

Current work moves across scenic fabrication, botanical sculpture, props, set dressing and immersive environments — from individual objects to spatial studies and large-scale visual pieces.

The approach is intentionally close and collaborative: concept, material testing and fabrication remain connected rather than separated into different departments.
