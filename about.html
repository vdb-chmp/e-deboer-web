ABOUT

A hands-on practice between matter, nature, fabrication and space.

E. DE BOER is a Spain-based Spatial Artist & Creative Fabricator developing handcrafted objects and environments for spaces that need character, atmosphere and physical presence.

A project may begin with a façade, a storefront, a terrace, an interior, a temporary installation or an individual piece. The approach remains the same: read the place, work close to the material and build something specific to its setting.

Raw surfaces, wood, metal, textiles and botanical forms become a visual language that can move between hospitality, retail, private spaces, events and visual production without belonging to only one industry.
